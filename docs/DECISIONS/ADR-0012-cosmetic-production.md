# ADR-0012: Original 300-cosmetic production plan

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Resolves:** RC-017
**Requirement:** `ZBW-READY-003`

## Decision

Produce five independently gated batches of 60 original or properly licensed cosmetics under `docs/COSMETIC_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md`. Every countable definition needs stable identity, material quality, preview/unlock/permission, provenance, fallbacks, accessibility, budgets and tests; clones/placeholders do not count.

## Consequences and controls

Content production is substantial and must be paced after renderer/registry foundations. Automated count/hash/schema/provenance gates plus human originality/quality review preserve the complete 300+ scope and prevent copied or low-value filler.
